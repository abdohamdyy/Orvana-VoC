# Customer Service Policy — Orvana Retail Group (short internal version)

## 1. Delivery
- Cairo and Giza: delivery within 48 hours of order confirmation.
- Other governorates: within 4 working days.
- Delay of more than 3 days past the promised date: the customer gets a 50 EGP voucher.
- Delay of more than 7 days: a 100 EGP voucher, and the customer may cancel the order for a full refund.
- One voucher per order only.

## 2. Returns and refunds
- Returns within 14 days of delivery, with the product in its original condition.
- Electronics: return within 7 days if there is a manufacturing defect; after that, the 1-year warranty is handled by the authorised dealer.
- Opened food and detergents cannot be returned, unless expired or defective.
- Card refunds: within 5 working days. Wallet or cash-on-delivery refunds: within 10 working days.
- Double charge: refunded within 3 working days after confirmation with the bank.

## 3. Prices and promotions
- If the shelf price is lower than the receipt price, the customer pays the lower price.
- Promotions are applied automatically at checkout and in the app, during the promotion period only.

## 4. Orvana Plus (loyalty program)
- 1 point per 10 EGP spent.
- Points appear in the account within 72 hours of purchase.
- Points are valid for 12 months.
- 100 points = 10 EGP discount.

## 5. Compensation and escalation
- No cash compensation through chat. Compensation is vouchers only, within the rules above.
- Any complaint about a product overheating, smelling burnt, or expired food: escalate to the Quality team within 2 hours, and the product is pulled from the store.
- Staff behaviour complaints: escalate to the store manager within 24 hours.
- First reply to any complaint within 24 hours.
